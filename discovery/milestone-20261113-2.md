## 1. What it is

A small Vercel site of independent strategy-game helpers. Live work is the AoE4 Build Order Simulator (English only), a
deterministic step simulation with saved builds, charts, comparison and an API. The WOTR Combat Simulator is the second app:
a Monte Carlo estimate of War of the Ring field-battle outcomes, "unfinished". StarCraft Helper is frozen. Out of scope: other
AoE4 civs, WOTR character/Nazgul leadership, building WOTR max rounds/retreat inside M1.

## 2. The field (read 2026-11-13)

- WOTR 2E rules (Dized rules pages): "Each die rolled scores a hit on a result of '5' or '6.'"; leader re-roll of failed dice up
  to Leadership, capped at five, same hit target. Casualties: per hit remove a Regular or replace an Elite with a Regular; the
  attacker chooses first. Against a City or Fortification the attacker hits only on 6 in the first round (per a First Edition
  FAQ, so verify in 2E). A besieged Army cannot retreat. https://rules.dized.com/game/QyWMz3nySiqXu-1OHZ9PIg/F-_3s3SFTNWtnU9BOIKNCQ/resolving-a-battle ;
  https://rulespal.com/war-of-the-ring-first-edition/faq/fortification-city-first-round
- Odds tools for other games (RISK, Axis and Allies, TripleA) compute exact odds by matrix or memoization where armies are
  small; TripleA notes simulation is used when exact calculation is too complex. No WOTR calculator found.
  https://ics.uci.edu/~sbartell/risk/about.riskodds.html ; https://www.axisandallies.org/forums/topic/5479/true-odds-calculator/21?page=1
- Build-order optimization is a mature research topic for StarCraft (BOSS by Churchill and Buro, branch and bound over a
  build-action simulator; SCFusion optimizer for SC2). BOSS is "a simulator of the game's build mechanics", the same shape as
  this AoE4 engine. I found no AoE build-order optimizer. https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AIIDE/article/view/12435 ;
  https://tl.net/forum/starcraft-2/168348-scfusion-wol-hots-and-lotv-build-order-optimizer

## 3. Gaps and openings

- Core WOTR rule mismatch: the simulator hits on 6 only; the game hits on 5 or 6. Every WOTR number the app shows is
  therefore too pessimistic for both sides (hit chance 1/6 vs 1/3 per die). Not covered by run 1 (its WOTR item assumed the
  dice rule was confirmed).
- WOTR sieges: the one battle config is an open-field fight; sieges (first-round 6 for the attacker, no retreat) are common
  game situations and not out of scope.
- WOTR exact odds: armies here are tiny (at most 10 units and 5 leaders per side), so the state space is small and exact
  probabilities are feasible; Monte Carlo output has sampling noise the page does not state.
- AoE4: the engine already plays a build; searching over builds for a goal is the field's standard next step (BOSS). Weigh
  against: provisional gather rates (run 1 item 1) make optimized results untrustworthy until accuracy is measured.
- Ruled out, not proposed: other civs, character/Nazgul leadership, StarCraft Helper.

## Items (strongest first)

1. **WOTR combat hits on 5 or 6, not 6 only (code and `docs/combat-simulator.md`).** Pipeline: standalone bug (fix doc and
   code in one PR; strong model, changes combat rules). Priority: high. Confidence: standard (the doc was owner-reviewed
   and says 6 on purpose, so the owner should confirm the rule; the rulebook text is unambiguous).
   Evidence: `lib/combat-simulator/battle.ts` `calculateCombatRollResults`: `threshold = opts.threshold || 5` and
   `if (thisDice > threshold)` hits only on 6. Doc: "2. **Hits.** A die hits on a 6 only." and "A re-rolled die hits on a 6
   only." and the mermaid line "Each 6 is a hit". Rulebook: "Each die rolled scores a hit on a result of '5' or '6.'" (Dized
   link above). The variable name `threshold = 5` with `>` looks like an off-by-one. Why: the app's whole output is wrong for
   every matchup. Cost of skipping: owner trusts odds that are off by a factor of two in hit rate.
2. **WOTR: siege battles (city, fortification, stronghold).** Pipeline: milestone. Priority: low-medium. Confidence: standard.
   Evidence: PROJECT.md says WOTR "runs one battle today"; Out of scope names only leadership and defers max rounds/retreat,
   not sieges; rules above (attacker hits on 6 in round 1, besieged cannot retreat; confirm in 2E rulebook). Why: sieges are
   where players most want odds, and it shares the retreat seam from run 1 item 4, so order after item 1 and consider
   bundling with it.
3. **WOTR: exact outcome probabilities (and stated sampling error).** Pipeline: milestone (small). Priority: low.
   Confidence: low. Evidence: army bounds in docs/combat-simulator.md Input bounds keep the state space small; RISK and
   Axis and Allies calculators compute exact odds (links above). Why: removes run-to-run noise and the 100,000-battle cost.
   Judgment: the owner may prefer to keep Monte Carlo, and later rules (cards, leadership) enlarge the state.
4. **AoE4: goal-driven build search (find a faster path to a target such as Castle Age or 100 villagers).**
   Pipeline: milestone. Priority: low. Confidence: low. Evidence: BOSS and SCFusion for StarCraft (links above); the engine
   is deterministic and bounded (docs/aoe4-build-order.md Determinism). Why: turns the tool from "test my opener" into "improve
   it". Defer until accuracy (run 1 item 1) is established.
