## 1. What it is

A small Vercel site of independent strategy-game helpers. The live work is the AoE4 Build Order Simulator (English only): a
deterministic step simulation of a build order with a JSON and paste format, step editor and repeat steps, saved and named builds
opened by link, pinned-run comparison, zoomable charts, and an OpenAPI-described API; since M10 it is a desktop-only page. It serves
the owner and people he shares links with who want to test openers without playing them. The WOTR Combat Simulator is a Monte Carlo
estimate of field-battle outcomes (unfinished, one battle configuration; the 5-or-6 hit rule is now fixed). StarCraft Helper is frozen.
Out of scope: non-English civs, WOTR character/Nazgul leadership, WOTR max rounds/retreat inside M1 (seams only, later ticket).
