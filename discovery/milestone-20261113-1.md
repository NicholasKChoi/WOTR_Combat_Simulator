## 1. What it is

A small Vercel-hosted site of independent strategy-game helpers. The live work is the AoE4 Build Order Simulator
(English only): a deterministic step-by-step simulation of an Age of Empires IV build order (villagers, gathering,
construction, research, age-ups) that reports macro timings, charts, and per-step waits, with a JSON and paste format,
saved and named builds opened by link, pinned-run comparison, and an OpenAPI-described API. It serves the owner (and
people he shares links with) who want to test and tune openers without playing them. The WOTR Combat Simulator is
the second app: a Monte Carlo estimate of War of the Ring field-battle outcomes (status: unfinished, one battle
configuration, untouched since early November's strictNullChecks pass). StarCraft Helper is frozen.
Out of scope: non-English civs, WOTR character/Nazgul leadership, building WOTR max-rounds/retreat inside M1 (seams only).

## 3. Gaps and openings

- Interchange: the sim has its own JSON/paste format; the community shares builds as aoe4guides/overlay files, so a user
  cannot bring a guide into the simulator or take a tuned build to the overlay. Not ruled out by scope.
- Accuracy is unmeasured: base gather rates are provisional (BACKLOG), techs' combat effects are ignored by design, and no
  test compares the sim to a real game. Replay/dump data exists (AoE4 World data dumps, replay analyzers). A simulator whose
  timings nobody can trust is not useful to share.
- Patch drift: the game patches roughly monthly and the snapshot refresh is a manual script. Users cannot see which patch
  a result reflects unless the page says so.
- Randomness: the AoE4 doc lists "spread of outcomes" as an extension seam, but the sim is deterministic; real games vary
  (travel, scouting, losses). Low value until accuracy is established.
- WOTR is the weak app: one battle config, no tool in the field, so low competition, but its doc already says "unfinished".
  Max rounds and retreat were deferred as seams, and "later ticket" (not ruled out).
- Ruled out, not proposed: other civs (Vikings/Scots/Jin included), WOTR character/Nazgul leadership, StarCraft Helper.
- Already on the backlog, not repeated: repeat-count steps, research occupying its building, auto-house planning, chart
  table/markers/overlap, rename saved build, English tech gaps, provisional gather rates.

## Items (strongest first)

1. **Measured accuracy: compare simulated timings with real games and replace provisional rates.** Pipeline: milestone.
   Priority: high. Evidence: BACKLOG "AoE4 base gather rates are provisional"; M2.2 overlay values carry "unverified"
   labels; no accuracy criterion in M1-M9; AoE4 World data dumps and replay analyzers exist as ground truth
   (https://aoe4world.com/tools). Why: every other feature is worth little if the timings are off, and this is what
   separates the tool from guide sites. Cost of skipping: owner keeps tuning builds against unverified numbers.
   Shape: pick 3-5 reference builds with known real timings, assert sim within a stated tolerance, cite patch.
2. **Import and export build orders in the community overlay/guide format.** Pipeline: milestone. Priority: medium.
   Evidence: aoe4guides supports AoE4_Overlay import/export (forum post above); sim only reads its own JSON/paste
   (docs/aoe4-build-order.md, text-to-build.ts). Why: lets users load any shared guide, see its charts and timings, and
   export a tuned build back to the in-game overlay. Open point: the overlay schema was not retrieved; the first ticket
   should fetch a real export and decide what maps to simulator steps (villager counts and notes map poorly to actions).
3. **Show and track the game patch on every result.** Pipeline: milestone (small; could be two standalone chores).
   Priority: medium. Evidence: snapshot `source.patch` 17.0.12182 exists but patches ship about monthly (16.2 in June,
   17.0 in October) and the refresh is manual (data/aoe4-build-order/README.md); 17.0 invalidates older replays. Why:
   saved and shared builds silently go stale, and the owner shares links. Shape: patch label in the results header and
   saved-build records, plus a periodic check that the snapshot matches the newest patch.
4. **WOTR: finish the battle model with max rounds and retreat.** Pipeline: milestone. Priority: low.
   Evidence: PROJECT.md Purpose ("unfinished; runs one battle today") and Out of scope (seams left for max rounds and
   retreat, response allows other outcome kinds); no purpose-built WOTR calculator found. Why: the app is the least
   developed of the live two and the seams are already in place. Honors the exclusion: no character or Nazgul leadership.
   Verify the rules against the WOTR 2E rulebook before specifying (I confirmed only the dice and re-roll rule).
5. **Spread of AoE4 outcomes (optional variance).** Pipeline: milestone, defer. Priority: low. Evidence: docs/aoe4-build-order.md
   Extension points row "A spread of outcomes" (runs and seed on SimulateRequest). Why: gives ranges instead of one number,
   but only worth it once item 1 shows where the model is off; no competitor offers it.
