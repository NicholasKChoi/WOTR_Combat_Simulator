## Blocker (needs_spec)

Two cases in `acceptance/aoe4-chart-layout.spec.ts` fail once the "AoE4 chart collapse and reorder" describe runs at the default viewport (1280x720), as the ticket requires:

- "ArrowUp on the first chart changes nothing, keeps focus and does not scroll the page"
- "ArrowDown on the last chart changes nothing, keeps focus and does not scroll the page" (same `for` loop, line ~160)

Failed assertion: `expect(before.y).toBeLessThan(before.max)` (line 168), received 245 against < 245. It is a precondition of the case. In the two-column layout the window's `scrollHeight` is 965 (probe at 1280x720) while the floating-resources chart sits at y=2348. So the charts scroll inside the Results column, not the window, and `window.scrollY` is pinned at its max after `scrollIntoView`. The case's "does not scroll the page" check can only be meaningful against the inner scroller there. At 1280x2800 the window cannot scroll at all, so a taller viewport does not fix it.

The ticket says every case not named keeps its assertions, so I changed none. Needed decision: have the case measure the Results column's scroll position (or drop the precondition), or run these two cases elsewhere. All other 98 cases pass. The branch is committed with the rest of the work.
