### A. Acceptance specs merged without ever running (escaped every local gate)

Evidence: SGS-113 (low risk) added the acceptance specs its ticket required. The implementer did not run
them (implementer.md:57 forbids authoring harness tests, yet the ticket required it), acceptance-test was
skipped as low risk, the suite ran unit only (suite.md runs commands.unit). #123 merged with
pin-compare.spec:141 failing; found later and fixed by a whole new ticket, SGS-126 / #130. SGS-118 and
SGS-126 were test-only acceptance diffs where acceptance-test was skipped and "the implementer's and
suite's runs are the evidence" (manage.md ~286-295), but the suite ran full acceptance in only 2 of 4
batches (batch-114-116-118-123 and batch-126-122-112-115; batch-113-117-119 and batch13 ran unit only).
review-model-assessment-1.md: the sonnet low-risk review of SGS-113 did not run the specs either.

Root cause / escape point: tooling gap. Since f2c25b4 only acceptance-test runs e2e, but the skip rules
still assume the implementer and suite run harness specs. Escape point: acceptance-test (skip rule).

Proposal (engine): Issue draft: reports/retro-20261111-issue-1.md -
"Never skip acceptance-test when a diff adds or edits acceptance specs"
