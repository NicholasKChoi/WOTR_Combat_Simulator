## Gaps

1. **Acceptance criteria (drop indicator) - the indicator has no observable position - an implementer must invent a hook, and the test cannot "read position from computed layout".** The ticket demands an inset/outer `box-shadow`, outline or pseudo-element (nothing with a bounding box) and also demands tests read its position "from the computed layout, not from an attribute". A shadow or pseudo-element has no `getBoundingClientRect`, so the test could only infer position from which tbody carries it, which is an implementation detail the ticket never names. Which element carries the indicator (the tbody below the slot, the one above, the table) is also undecided, and the first/last slots need a different rule. Proposed fix: state the contract. Either (a) one indicator element with `data-testid="chart-drop-indicator"`, absolutely positioned inside a `position: relative` wrapper of the table (outside the table, so no row or layout box changes), rendered only during an active drag, with its top computed from the same measured group rects; criteria then compare its `getBoundingClientRect()` y and width against the group boxes read in the same `evaluate` (between third bottom and fourth top, spanning the table's width); or (b) name the carrier tbody and edge and the computed-style property the test reads. Option (a) also removes the unlisted Chromium assumptions about box-shadow on `<tbody>` under `border-collapse`.

2. **Scope and criteria (focus tint during and after a drag) - contradictory and underspecified - an implementer would guess.** `start()` does not preventDefault pointerdown, so Chromium focuses the dragged handle on press. With `:focus-within` the dragged header row is therefore tinted for the whole drag, yet the criterion "No hover tint appears on any header row while a drag is active" and Scope ("no hover tint on top of the drag styling") do not say whether the focus tint is suppressed on the dragged row. After the drop, focus is moved programmatically to the handle, so the moved row keeps a tint with the pointer elsewhere, and a mouse click on the caret or view toggle leaves a stuck tint too, while the Focus criterion only talks about keyboard focus. Proposed fix: state the rule. Suggested: tint on `:focus-within` only when the focus is keyboard-visible (`:has(:focus-visible)`), or accept the stuck tint explicitly; and say the dragged row shows no tint of either kind while `data-dragging` is set. Make the "no tint during drag" criterion read "no header row's background differs from resting".

3. **Acceptance criteria (contrast) - "legible contrast ... a test or the implementer's recorded check" is not observable - an implementer has no pass bar.** Proposed fix: "the computed text colour of the title and of the buttons against the tinted background has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 in a light and in a dark colour scheme, computed in a test with `page.emulateMedia({ colorScheme })` or the `data-bs-theme` mechanism the page uses". Confirm during writing that the page actually has a dark scheme mechanism; if it does not, drop the dark half rather than leave it untestable.

## Notes (non-blocking)

- `setState` on every `pointermove` re-renders `Results` and every recharts `SeriesChart` per move. Consider keeping the offset and drop index in the hook but applying the transform and indicator without re-rendering the charts (a ref-set style, or memoized `SeriesChart`), or at least say frame-rate jank is not a criterion. The offset is a pointer-to-start `clientY` difference; a page scroll during the drag (wheel) shifts the group's rect without changing it, so the displayed offset drifts from the pointer. Out-of-scope already lists auto-scroll; say what happens meanwhile.
- `ChartControls` gains fields. The three unit-test literals (`chart-header.test.ts:22`, `line-chart.test.ts:27`, `:155`) will fail `tsc` if the new fields are required; make them optional or list those edits (the ticket's "lists every test it edits" covers it).
- The pointer-capture hover claim: with capture, `:hover` matches the handle's ancestors, including the dragged `tr`, so the suppression is needed on the dragged row (the ticket covers this); the other rows are correctly un-hovered.
- Unverified assumptions list covers the transform and box-shadow cases; if gap 1 option (a) is taken, drop the box-shadow items.
