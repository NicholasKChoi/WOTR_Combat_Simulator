## Gaps

1. Item 1 (edges, result shape) - "the villager series ends at the order's produce count" is false at the base. The default continuous villager order (step `null`, capped by `base.villagerTarget`) keeps paying after a `produce villager` step finishes. `start 6 sheep` plus `produce villager from capital-town-center x3` at the default 1200 s ends with a villager series of 23, not 3. The deleted test's `3` is stale (probably why the file was deleted). Re-spec 3 re-verified the schema sentence but not the engine. An implementer writes `toBe(3)` and the test fails. Fix: "the villager series' last count is at least the produce count (an input) and the series never decreases; the default villager order keeps producing after the step, so the last count may exceed it". Rewrite the acceptance bullet "Item 1 (respec 3)" the same way (it currently says the test fails if the series does not end at the produce count).

2. Item 7 (spend-population) - `spent.villagers` equals 40 times the villager cost, and the precondition "villager series ends at 40", both fail at the base. 40 villagers from the step plus the continuous order give a series ending at 43 and `spent.villagers` 2150 (43 x 50); `spent.buildings` 200, `military` 0, `research` 0 (so the buildings and zero parts hold). The deleted test's `40 * cost` is stale. Fix: precondition: the villager series' last count is at least 40 (the step's units are all paid and spawned by the default horizon); then `spent.villagers` is a whole multiple of the villager cost (from the snapshot), is at least the villager series' last count times that cost (continuous producers pay ahead), and `spent.buildings` is positive and a whole multiple of the house cost; `military` and `research` end at 0. Update the "Item 7 (respec 3)" criterion to match.

3. Item 6(a) (depletion-engine, berry stock) - "the `floating.food` series rises by exactly the stock, because nothing is spent" is false. With the deleted setup (stock 601, steps `[PAUSE]`), `floating.food` goes 150 to 651 (rise 501), because the default villager order pays 150 food (`spent.villagers` 150). The deleted `toBeCloseTo(AMOUNT)` would fail. Fix: assert the rule on `gathered.food`: its last sample minus its first (0) equals the stock (601 holds today), or `floating.food` rise plus `spent.villagers` last equals the stock. Reword the criterion ("fails if the `floating` series ... rises by the stock") accordingly. The `depletedAt` not a multiple of `sampleSeconds` part holds (365.0625, 5 s samples).

4. Item 6(b) (idle-arrival) - "`idleVillagers` equals the produce `count`, exactly one warning" fails at the default horizon. With the deleted setup (count 2, `rally: 'berry'`, `walkSeconds: 5`, `nodeAmounts: { berry: 0 }`, default horizon), the one `idle-arrival` row has `idleVillagers` 3: the producer's rally persists, so the continuous order's next villager also arrives at berry (series ends at 3). At `durationSeconds` 60 it is 2. The warning's `idleVillagers` is "total arrived by durationSeconds". Fix: assert there is exactly one `idle-arrival` row for berry, and `idleVillagers` is at least the produce count and at most the final villager series count (every villager produced after the rally change goes to the dry node); precondition `count` above 1. Do not state equality.

5. Item 6(c) (numbered gold deposits) - "deposit 2's depletion row deeply equal to the run where both are finite" fails by one ulp. Fix: `node` and `amount` equal, and `depletedAt` within 1e-6 (floating point tolerance); deposit 1's row `amount` null and `depletedAt` null holds.
